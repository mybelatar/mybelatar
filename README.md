## My Approach

I focus on AI systems that solve real business problems.

My approach is based on five principles:

1. **Business value first**  
   AI should improve revenue, efficiency, visibility, service quality or decision-making.

2. **Practical implementation**  
   A useful workflow is more valuable than an impressive but unusable demonstration.

3. **Human oversight**  
   Important decisions, communications and outputs should remain reviewable and controllable.

4. **Clear measurement**  
   Every AI initiative should have understandable objectives and measurable results.

5. **Local relevance**  
   AI solutions must reflect the language, market, culture and operational reality of the organization.
